1 · Add it to your home screen

  1. Open this page in Safari. Other iPhone browsers can't install it or show notifications. If you're in Chrome, copy {{ request.host }}/staff into Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (at the top on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen” . If you don't see it, tap “Edit Actions” at the bottom of the list and add it.
  4. Tap “Add” in the top-right corner. The Codencode Staff icon appears on your home screen.
  5. Close Safari and open the app from the new icon. Sign in once more. Notifications only work when it is opened from the icon (iOS 16.4 or later).

2 · Turn on notifications

  1. Open the app from your home-screen icon (on iPhone this is required).
  2. Tap then “Allow”.
  3. You'll be reminded 15 minutes before each class, if you haven't clocked in 10 minutes after the start, and if you forget to clock out.
I tapped “Don't Allow” by mistake

iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Codencode Staff → turn on Allow Notifications.
Android: long-press the app icon → App info → Notifications → turn on.
Computer: click the lock icon next to the address → Notifications → Allow.

3 · Using it every day

  1. Zoom class: open My classes and tap Start class (it appears 15 minutes before). It clocks you in and opens Zoom. In Zoom, rename yourself to “Your name – Teacher”.
  2. Office work: at the office, open Office clock-in, type your task, tap Check my location, then Clock in. Allow location when asked.
  3. Finished? Tap the green Clock out bar at the top of any page.
  4. Check your hours on Attendance and My pay. Green = verified. Amber or red = admin will review it. Dashed = waiting for the Zoom report.
You can't edit a clock-in after the fact. If something is wrong, tell admin; they can correct it.
