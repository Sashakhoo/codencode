Why 2 anchor + 4 short works cleanly
Python and ML run the full 10-week cohort — they are the backbone. Every cohort opens and closes around them. Students who only want Python or ML get a complete, well-paced experience.
The 4 short tracks (Vibe, No-code, Web Dev, Data Analytics) start on Day 1 but finish 4–6 weeks in. Their students graduate mid-cohort with certificates, without waiting for the full 10-week window. This also means 4 separate Saturday slots free up in the second half of each cohort.

Key improvement from dropping Vibe Coding to 4 weeks
Cleaner grouping. Vibe Coding now sits naturally alongside No-code AI — both are 4-week fast-delivery courses for builders who want results fast. They share the same Saturday morning block and the same student profile.
Reduced teacher load. Saturday drops from 8.5 hrs (old) to 7 hrs (new) — Vibe Coding frees up a 1.5-hr slot in weeks 5–10. That's 9 fewer live sessions per cohort across 3 cohorts = 27 fewer sessions per year.
Better student experience. A 4-week course that ships a live app feels faster and more satisfying than 10 weeks of it. Students get their certificate, a real project, and momentum — which is exactly the Vibe Coding promise.
