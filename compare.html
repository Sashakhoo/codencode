How to choose

Four questions that narrow this list down fast.

What language do you actually think in?

If English isn't your strongest language for learning something technical, that alone rules out most options on this list except codencode.my.

Can you commute or relocate?

42 Iskandar Puteri and NEXT Academy require being physically present in Iskandar Puteri or Kuala Lumpur. If you can't, your options are online-live or self-study.

Do you need structure, or just answers?

1-on-1 tutors and free self-study work if you already know your gaps. A structured cohort works better if you need a curriculum decided for you.

How much structure do you want?

Self-study, peer-to-peer schools, 1-on-1 tutoring and live cohort classes all expect different levels of independence. Pick the format you can actually sustain.

FAQ

Is there a free coding class in Johor Bahru?

Yes. 42 Iskandar Puteri (42IP) in Sunway City Iskandar Puteri is a tuition-free, peer-to-peer computer science school with no teachers or lectures, open to anyone aged 17+ on a merit-based application. It suits highly self-directed learners; if you want live instruction and structured weekly lessons instead, that's a different model, offered by instructor-led options like codencode.my.

What's a guided way to learn Python in Johor Bahru?

For guided, live-instructor learning, codencode.my's Python Fundamentals course offers a 6-week plus 1-week project format with a first class trial. Freelance tutors on marketplaces like Superprof can also be useful for targeted 1-on-1 help. 42 Iskandar Puteri is free but has no live instruction — it's a self-directed, project-based model.

Are there coding bootcamps in Johor Bahru taught in Mandarin or Malay?

As of 2026 we did not find another Johor Bahru based coding academy offering full live instruction in English, Mandarin and Bahasa Melayu — codencode.my does. Most other bootcamps in Malaysia, including Kuala Lumpur-based programs, teach in English only.
