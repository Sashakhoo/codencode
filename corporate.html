About

Your Corporate AI &
Digital Transformation Training Partner

Codencode Academy is a Malaysia-based training provider specialising in artificial intelligence, coding, business automation and applied digital skills.

We help organisations equip employees with practical technology skills that improve productivity, streamline operations and support digital transformation.

Our programmes combine instructor-led learning, practical demonstrations, hands-on exercises, real-world business scenarios and structured assessments.

Why Corporate AI Training?

CCA-001AI for Workplace Productivity1 Day / 7 Hours

Duration: 1 Day / 7 Hours

Target participants: Employees, executives, administrative personnel, supervisors and managers.

Course overview

This programme introduces practical generative AI applications that help employees improve communication, documentation, research, planning and everyday workplace productivity.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the programme, participants will be able to:

  1. Explain common workplace applications and limitations of generative AI.
  2. Construct effective prompts for business tasks.
  3. Prepare professional business documents using AI.
  4. Use AI to support research, summarisation and planning.
  5. Apply responsible AI practices when handling business information.

Course modules

Module 1: Introduction to Generative AI — 1 hour

  • Understanding generative AI
  • Common AI applications
  • Workplace opportunities and limitations
  • Responsible AI usage

Module 2: Effective Prompt Engineering — 1.5 hours

  • Prompt structures
  • Context and instructions
  • Improving response accuracy
  • Reusable business prompts

Module 3: AI for Business Communication — 1.5 hours

  • Professional emails
  • Reports and proposals
  • Meeting summaries
  • Document improvement

 

Module 4: AI for Research & Planning — 1.5 hours

  • Information gathering
  • Summarisation
  • Business planning
  • AI-assisted brainstorming

Module 5: Workplace Productivity Challenge — 1.5 hours

  • Practical business scenario
  • AI-assisted task completion
  • Output validation
  • Group discussion

Practical deliverable: AI Workplace Productivity Toolkit.

Assessment: Practical exercises and workplace task.
